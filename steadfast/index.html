Steadfast

Stay steady. Block what pulls you away.

Porn blocking

Adult websites are filtered for the whole phone through a family-safe DNS filter, with SafeSearch forced on Google and Bing and YouTube in Restricted Mode.

Your block list

Pick social media, games or any other app. Steadfast closes it whenever it opens.

Daily limits

Give an app a number of minutes per day. When the time is used up, it is blocked until midnight.

Hard to switch off

Turning protection off needs a cool-down period and then your PIN, so a weak moment passes before anything changes. Making things stricter always works straight away.

Private by design

Everything Steadfast knows stays on your phone. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours. Read the privacy policy.
